CVPRProceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)2026

Personalized Federated Training of Diffusion Models with Privacy Guarantees

Kumar Kshitij Patel*, Bingqing Jiang*, A F M Mahfuzul Kabir*, Weitong Zhang, Difan Zou, and Lingxiao Wang

Abstract

We propose a federated framework for training diffusion models on decentralized and private datasets. The method learns a shared generative model alongside personalized client models, allowing clients to benefit from cross-client structure while ensuring that the shared model cannot reproduce any client’s data on its own. We provide formal differential privacy guarantees for each client and establish utility bounds for conditional generation under a Gaussian mixture model, showing that collaboration improves sample quality relative to private non-collaborative training. Experiments on CIFAR-10, Colorized MNIST, and CelebA support these results: the method generates high-fidelity samples, improves performance on minority and underrepresented classes, and maintains strong protection against membership inference, memorization, and reconstruction attacks.

* Equal contribution · pp. 31790–31801

ICML FoGenICML 2026 Workshop on Foundations of Deep Generative Models: Understanding Memorization, Generalization, and Reasoning2026

Personalized Federated Training of Latent Diffusion Models with Privacy Guarantees

A F M Mahfuzul Kabir and Lingxiao Wang

Abstract

We study the problem of federated training of diffusion models (DMs) with privacy guarantees. For high-dimensional data, existing private federated DMs often exhibit a poor privacy-utility tradeoff, since the privacy noise introduced to protect client data becomes increasingly damaging as the dimension of the image representation grows. To address this challenge, we propose Personalized Federated Training of Latent Diffusion Models (PF-LDM), a framework that performs private federated diffusion training in latent space. The key idea is to identify latent representations whose distributional structure is more favorable for private learning. In particular, we show that latent spaces with more discriminative feature representations can better preserve utility under privacy constraints. PF-LDM further combines a shared server-side diffusion model with personalized client-side refinement models: the server captures cross-client generative structure from privatized latent data, while client-specific models refine samples to recover fine-grained local details. Experiments on the CelebAHQ dataset demonstrate that our method enables high-dimensional image generation, improves performance on underrepresented classes across clients, and maintains strong privacy protection.

FoGen 2026 · ICML workshop · Non-archival

IEEE ICTP2023 IEEE International Conference on Telecommunications and Photonics (ICTP)2023

mTOVA: A Multilingual Task Oriented Virtual Assistant for Human Computer Communication

Sabbir Hossain Ujjal*, A F M Mahfuzul Kabir*, and Mohammad Ariful Haque

Abstract

A task-oriented virtual assistant (VA) refers to an artificial intelligence-driven system that can assist users to perform daily activities. The utilisation of deep learning algorithms has enabled VAs to attain noteworthy advancements in high-resource languages, such as English. However, languages with limited resources, such as Bengali, have not encountered significant advancements in this domain. In this paper, we propose a multilingual task oriented voice-to-voice conversational agent, which is capable of proficiently managing diverse tasks such as weather forecast, date & time query, hospital and blood bank search etc in both Bengali and English language. Our developed system can understand voice command using Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Natural Language Understanding Unit (NLU). Then the system generates an appropriate reply by gathering information from the internet via APIs, data retrieval techniques and by employing dialogue management. Finally, Natural Language Generator (NLG) and Text to Speech (TTS) techniques are used to construct and deliver proper response. We integrated all the units using RASA framework and python script. Our developed ASR system has an average word error rate of 13% and NLU system has an intent and entity extraction accuracy of 93% and 96.2% respectively. The overall action prediction accuracy of our developed system is 99.4%

* Equal contribution · IEEE · pp. 01–05 · DOI: 10.1109/ICTP60248.2023.10490454

Current Research Federated Learning for Privacy Preserving Diffusion Modeling Diffusion Models Federated Learning Privacy-preserving ML
Principal investigator
Lingxiao Wang, Assistant Professor, Department of Data Science, New Jersey Institute of Technology

Diffusion generative models require abundant data to perform well, but they are especially prone to memorization. Although publicly available data are often curated for high-performance generative modeling, doing so is not viable for sensitive organizations in healthcare, finance, and law. Federated learning can support joint training without sharing raw data across clients, but researchers have increasingly shown that withholding client data alone does not automatically guarantee privacy. In fact, sharing gradients can result in privacy leakage through even simple attacks. Therefore, this research aims to redefine the diffusion process through the lens of federated learning. Rather than distributing training optimization across clients, we investigate methods for distributing the sampling process in diffusion modeling between global servers and local private clients. So far, our research has achieved this novelty with strong privacy guarantees in standard diffusion modeling, while modern latent diffusion and flow matching remain to be evaluated.

Undergraduate thesis Multilingual Conversational Agent in Low-Resource Domain NLP Speech Recognition Deep Learning
Thesis
Development of an end-to-end voice controlled multilingual conversational agent using deep learning and natural language processing
Principal investigator
Dr. Mohammad Ariful Haque, Professor, Department of EEE, BUET

This research deals with the unique challenges of voice-controlled Bengali conversational agents as it moves toward multilinguality as it's core achievement. The Bengali language in the deep learning domain has been infected with low data availability, and this research progresses toward that with unique appraoches. We used transformer-based architectures coupled with self-supervised learning to overcome data limitation in Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), and Natural Language Understanding. Massive pre-processing was also needed to tackle unique challenges of Bengali Unicode system for better performance. The resulting system empowered users to interact through voice commands in Bengali at ease.
